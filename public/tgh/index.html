Our Approach

What We're Delivering

  • 8 podcast episodes produced on-site at Tampa General Hospital
  • Director/producer, DP, audio engineer, and camera operator every session
  • 3-camera production with lighting, audio, live switching, and recording
  • On-camera talent support and full technical direction

Option A — Condensed Shoot

  • Load in once, establish the full lighting and audio workflow
  • Shoot all 8 episodes over 3 consecutive days
  • Eliminates repeated setup costs entirely
  • Consistent look and feel across every episode

Option B — Weekly Cadence

  • One episode per week over 8 weeks
  • Core infrastructure (cables, switcher, rigging, lighting base) stays installed at TGH between sessions where the facility allows
  • Each weekly session becomes a leaner load-in — cameras and select lighting travel with crew
  • Setup and strike details confirmed after location scout
