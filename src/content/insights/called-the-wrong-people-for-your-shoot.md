Someone with the latest iPhone, a good eye, and great editing software could bill themselves as a "production company." The label doesn't mean much on its own. Knowing who you actually need for your next video project, and calling the right people, saves time, money, and a lot of frustration on set.

Here's how to tell them apart.

## When a Videographer Is the Right Call

You need a videographer when your in-house team has already handled the creative: approved script, production plan, and editorial direction are all internal. You just need a professional to capture the content so your team can edit it.

The camera operator executes someone else's vision. That's the job, and it's a legitimate one. But if you're calling a videographer expecting them to bring strategy, direction, and production management, you've called the wrong people.

## When You Need Production Services

Production services means the company provides crew, equipment, and logistics, but you or your agency are still driving the creative. They're executing your concept, not developing it.

This is the right call when you have a strong creative department and need a reliable execution partner. You're not paying for ideation. You're paying for operational support.

## When You Need a Full Production Company

A full creative production company takes the brief and runs with it. Strategy, scripting, pre-production, casting, location scouting, directing, production management, post: the whole pipeline.

This is what you need when the project requires original creative development, when there's no in-house team to carry the concept, or when the stakes are high enough that execution risk needs to be managed by people who do this for a living.

## The Mistake That Costs You

The expensive mistake isn't hiring a production company when you needed a videographer. It's the reverse. You bring in a one-person crew expecting a full production and then spend the next three weeks trying to fix in post what should have been solved in pre-production.

Know what you're buying before you make the call.
