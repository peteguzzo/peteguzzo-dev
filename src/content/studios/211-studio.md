211 Studio started as an office building. What it is now is a creative operational production hub for commercial shoots, film, flexible production, and co-work, built by connecting offices, studios, a workshop, and support spaces into one workflow instead of leaving them as disconnected rooms.

## What got built

A structured workflow connecting offices, studios, workshop, and support spaces. An integrated set-building workshop, HMU salon, and post-production infrastructure, wired into a single system instead of bolted together after the fact. A space that transformed an office-based building into a multi-department creative production system.

## What's inside

- **Multi-level creative work space**, open-access environments designed for teams, clients, and productions
- **Event & gathering spaces**, a large second-floor event area plus flexible environments for activations and productions
- **Agency & production workspace**, up to 30 desks with terrace access
- **Private & collaborative areas**, conference room, huddle spaces, and quiet work environments
- **Workshop & build space**, indoor/outdoor workshop for set construction and fabrication
- **Hair, makeup & wardrobe salon**, full-service, production-ready, with a shower and dedicated wardrobe space
- **Studios & offices**, multiple studios, offices, and hidden workspaces designed for flexibility
- **Post-production integration**, catwalk offices wired for post, with connectivity across the entire facility
- **Shared amenities + private office wing**, a community kitchen and support spaces for full production days, plus a separate entrance with dedicated offices and access to key areas
- **Parking & access**, large parking capacity with direct access to downtown Tampa and major highways

## What it's been used for

Full production base for the episodic series *A Land Remembered*, offices, woodshop, wardrobe, grip & electric, and post, all under one roof. 50+ commercial shoots. Simultaneous multi-team production running across departments at the same time. That's the real test of whether a studio works as a system: can three different productions run in it on the same day without stepping on each other.

## The outcome

A flexible studio system that evolved from a commercial content space into a full-scale production environment.
