**Role:** Director & Producer · **Writer (with George Fuller)**

Made with Clearwater Police and the State of Florida, this documentary had a real job to do, train officers and raise public awareness on a subject that doesn't forgive sloppy or sensationalized handling. It won a National Addy, but the bar that mattered was whether it was actually useful in the field.
